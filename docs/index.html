Eligibility & signals

A stock must pass every eligibility, trend, and momentum rule. When SPY is below its 200-day average, stock results remain visible but the research portfolio stays in cash.

03

Ranking formula

Score = 0.60 × percentile(M6) + 0.40 × percentile(M12)

Percentiles are calculated only among stocks that qualify on the same date. Ties use average ranks. A single qualifying stock receives a neutral score of 50.

04

Track-record rules

Formal selections are frozen on each month’s final trading session and entered at the next session’s opening price. The model selects up to ten stocks at 10% target weights, with no more than two per sector. Unfilled weight stays in cash. The displayed simulation includes 10 basis points per transaction side.
