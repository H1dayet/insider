02 · Filters

Eligibility and signals

A stock must pass every eligibility, trend, and momentum rule. When SPY is below its 200-day average, results remain visible but the research portfolio stays in cash.

03 · Score

Ranking formula

Score = 0.60 × percentile(M6) + 0.40 × percentile(M12)

Percentiles use only stocks that qualify on the same date. Ties use average ranks.

04 · Simulation

Track-record rules

The forward-only paper portfolio starts on the displayed inception date with $10,000 and no historical backfill. It rebalances at each session’s opening to the prior completed session’s top ten qualified stocks, using 10% targets and no more than two stocks per sector. Unfilled weight stays in cash. The simulation includes 10 basis points per transaction side.
